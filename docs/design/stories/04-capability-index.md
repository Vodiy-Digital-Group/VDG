# Story 04 — Capability index

## User story

As a visitor with a concrete need, I want to see where VDG can help without reading a long sales catalogue.

## Scope

- Replace service cards with a compact, editorial capability index.
- Present four areas: Digital products & platforms; Systems & operations software; Product design systems; Technical discovery & delivery strategy.
- Reveal a concise explanatory line and a small schematic detail on interaction where useful.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each capability is a horizontal row with an index, title, concise description, and disclosure affordance.
- Hover/focus expands or highlights the same information; touch devices show content without hover dependency.
- The section fits in roughly one desktop viewport and uses dividers rather than repeated card containers.
- Blue appears only on the selected/active row and its structural mark.
- Copy describes services generically and truthfully; it does not imply clients, outcomes, or unsupported credentials.

## Out of scope

- Pricing tables, technology-logo clouds, certification claims, or project examples.

## Dependencies

- Story 01 tokens and layout.
