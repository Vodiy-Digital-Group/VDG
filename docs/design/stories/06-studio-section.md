# Story 06 — Studio section and operating principles

## User story

As a prospective partner, I want to know how VDG works and who I am working with so I can assess fit.

## Scope

- Replace abstract standards dashboards with an accountable studio statement.
- Present three operating principles: direct access, durable systems, and clear ownership.
- Include location/collaboration context in plain language.
- Use a typographic composition or a real studio/process artifact; do not use AI-generated people or office images.

## Acceptance criteria

- The copy is specific but makes no claims that cannot be substantiated.
- Principles are readable as content first, with any visual treatment secondary.
- The section has one dominant visual anchor and does not mimic a SaaS compliance matrix.
- If no real photograph/artifact is available, the design remains complete with type and structure alone.
- The visual anchor has descriptive alternative text when it communicates information.

## Out of scope

- Team bios, client quotes, awards, partner badges, or location maps without approved content.
