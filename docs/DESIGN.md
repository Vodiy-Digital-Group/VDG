## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-end engineering authority, Swiss editorial discipline, and digital avant-garde craftsmanship. Built for a high-tier digital product studio and IT enterprise, it projects uncompromised technical competence, architectural clarity, and forward-looking momentum.

The aesthetic is characterized as **Architectural Minimalist with Electric Accents**:

- Deep void grounds (`#0B0B0D`) provide an infinite canvas for precise light distribution.
- Rigid, razor-sharp 1px structural framing organizes complex data structures and bespoke editorial narratives.
- Sparse, surgical applications of electric blue (`#4361FF`) evoke high-voltage energy, engineered focus, and precision tooling.
- Angular treatments and diagonal motifs subtly pay homage to structural monogram geometry, avoiding superficial decorative clutter in favor of systematic, purposeful accents.
- Generous spatial margins create breathing room, turning every interface into a clean, gallery-grade experience.

## Colors

The color system enforces a dark-first hierarchy where contrast is managed through strictly controlled tonal layers and precise illumination:

- **Void (Base Surface):** `#0B0B0D` serves as the primary canvas, anchoring the entire interface in pure deep-space black.
- **Surface Elevation Containers:**
  - `surface-100` (`#121216`): Subtle elevation for baseline section partitioning.
  - `surface-200` (`#1A1A1E`): Interactive surfaces, inset panels, card bases.
  - `surface-300` (`#2A2A30`): Hover tiers, active controls, and elevated overlays.
- **Structural Outlines & Grids:** `#23232A` (1px precision borders and divider lines).
- **Typography & Content Tokens:**
  - `text-primary`: `#F5F5F3` — Crisp off-white offering high-contrast legibility without ocular fatigue.
  - `text-secondary`: `#A1A1AA` — Neutral mid-tone for subheadings, captions, and secondary metadata.
  - `text-muted`: `#71717A` — De-emphasized structural text, timestamps, and inactive iconography.
- **Accent (Electric Voltage):** `#4361FF` is reserved exclusively for dominant calls-to-action, high-priority system states, focused input borders, and radiant energy points. Use with disciplined restraint—it should never exceed 5% of total viewport area.
- **Electric Blue Glow Soft:** `rgba(67, 97, 255, 0.15)` for ambient backlights and structural ring indicators.

## Typography

The typography strategy unites Swiss modernist clarity with hyper-contemporary digital engineering:

- **Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Delivers sculptural, oversized presence with distinct geometric character. Tight letter-spacing reinforces structural rigidity, creating dramatic architectural tension against generous negative space.
- **Body Text (Hanken Grotesk):** Provides pristine, undistracted legibility across complex editorial texts, technical descriptions, and dense narrative blocks.
- **Labels, Metadata, & System Tags (JetBrains Mono):** Injects raw technical precision. Always rendered with positive tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) and predominantly in uppercase to differentiate operational data points from editorial copy.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a 12-column architectural fluid grid governed by absolute horizontal and vertical alignments:

- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, max canvas width of `1440px`, with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and outer margins of `3rem` (`48px`). In bespoke hero layouts, outer margins expand to `6rem` (`96px`) to cultivate editorial grandeur.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` gutters, `2rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` outer margins.
- **Grid Enclosure & Separators:** Layout sections are systematically cordoned off by 1px continuous borders (`#23232A`). Columns frequently reveal their vertical guides as structural lines behind modules to evoke blueprint schematics.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Strict multiples of `8px` (`0.5rem`) dictate spacing. Component heights are fixed to systematic metric heights: `32px` (compact), `40px` (standard), `48px` (prominent), and `56px` (hero controls).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained without synthetic drop shadows or blurred skeuomorphic skews. Instead, depth is articulated through **Tonal Stratification and Radiance Fields**:

- **Layer 0 (Ground):** Base page viewport in `#0B0B0D`.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** Inset containers colored in `#121216` with a full-perimeter 1px hairline border of `#23232A`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Overlays & Popovers):** Elevated elements in `#1A1A1E` with `#2A2A30` structural borders, accompanied by an ultra-diffused atmospheric glow: `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.
- **Focused & Interactive State (Electric Voltage Radiance):** Interactive elements do not receive heavy drop shadows. Instead, they produce a tight, technical light bloom using electric blue: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #4361FF, 0 0 20px rgba(67, 97, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Structural Diagonal Framing:** Strategic component headers and cards leverage chamfered accents or 45-degree corner ticks rendered via SVG or CSS clip-paths, referencing the geometric DNA of the monogram.

## Shapes

The shape system is deliberately low-radius (`roundedness: 1`), embodying technical precision and structural discipline over playful softness:

- **Default Radii:**
  - Base structural containers, input frames, and buttons utilize `0.25rem` (`4px`).
  - Cards, modals, and panel wraps utilize `0.5rem` (`8px`).
  - High-density pills, status chips, and mono labels utilize `0.125rem` (`2px`) or strictly hard `0px` corners to preserve industrial clarity.
- **Geometric Monogram Cutouts:** Interactive focus plates and decorative badge containers may employ micro-chamfered upper-right or lower-left corner cutouts (3px to 4px angle), reinforcing the structural identity of the studio.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary Action:** Solid `#4361FF` background, text `#F5F5F3` in `Space Grotesk` (Weight 500, 14px), radius `4px`. Hover triggers a brightness shift (`#5A75FF`) and an ambient bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(67, 97, 255, 0.35)`. Transition: `150ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
- **Secondary (Outline / Technical):** Background transparent, 1px border `#23232A`, text `#F5F5F3`. Hover shifts border to `#71717A` and background to `#1A1A1E`.
- **Ghost (Mono):** `JetBrains Mono`, uppercase, text `#A1A1AA`. Hover shifts text to `#4361FF` with an animated underline or trailing directional arrow `->`.

### Chips & Badges

- Displayed in `JetBrains Mono` (`label-mono-sm`), uppercase with `0.08em` tracking.
- Height: `22px`. Padding: `0 8px`.
- Background `#1A1A1E`, border `1px solid #23232A`, text `#A1A1AA`.
- Live/Status active indicator: 6px pulsing dot `#4361FF` with `box-shadow: 0 0 8px #4361FF`.

### Input Fields

- Height: `44px`. Background `#121216`, border `1px solid #23232A`, radius `4px`.
- Typography: `Hanken Grotesk` 14px, text `#F5F5F3`, placeholder `#71717A`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#4361FF` with an inner/outer hairline glow ring (`0 0 0 1px #4361FF, 0 0 12px rgba(67, 97, 255, 0.2)`).
- Input labels rendered in `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase above the field.

### Cards

- Surface base `#121216`, wrapped in a crisp `1px solid #23232A` border, corner radius `8px`.
- Generous internal padding (`space-xl` / `32px`).
- Hover interaction: Hairline border shifts smoothly from `#23232A` to `#2A2A30` with subtle top-edge illumination via a fine linear gradient overlay (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(67, 97, 255, 0.08) 0%, transparent 40%)`).

### Lists & Tables

- Row dividers: `1px solid #23232A`.
- Row headers: `JetBrains Mono` (`label-mono-sm`), text `#71717A`, uppercase.
- Interactive rows shift to `#1A1A1E` on hover with a 2px vertical electric blue `#4361FF` accent line anchored to the far-left border edge.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls

- Base: `16px x 16px` square, border `1px solid #2A2A30`, background `#121216`, radius `2px` (checkbox) or circular (radio).
- Checked: Background `#4361FF`, border `#4361FF`, with `#F5F5F3` icon indicator. Focus adds a `2px` ring of `rgba(67, 97, 255, 0.3)`.

### Studio-Specific Accent: Technical Metric Stat Display

- Oversized numeric metric in `Space Grotesk` (`headline-xl`, `#F5F5F3`).
- Subtitle locked underneath in `JetBrains Mono` (`label-mono-sm`, `#71717A`), framed by an upper 1px hairline divider with an integrated electric blue corner node.
